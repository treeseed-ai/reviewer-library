outcome: "TreeSeed Reviewer separates product guarantees from cleanup evidence and reports only what exact fixtures and checks prove."

TreeSeed Reviewer provides independent, reproducible verification and truthful evidence for TreeSeed changes and releases.

Guarantee evidence, cleanup evidence, and fixture identity must remain distinct. A blocked prerequisite cannot be reported as a passed guarantee.

TreeDX is the sole authoring and publication authority for this objective.
